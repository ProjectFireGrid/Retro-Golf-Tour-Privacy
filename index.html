RETRO GOLF TOUR

Privacy Policy

Effective October 8, 2026 · iOS TestFlight beta

Joseph Wells develops Retro Golf Tour. This policy explains how information is handled in the current iOS beta and when you contact us or visit this policy page.

Game data on your device

The game stores career progress, player names you enter, preferences, round state and course caches locally on your device. The current native beta does not require an account or send this local game data to a developer-operated server. Your operating system may include app data in device backups according to your settings.

Analytics, ads and device permissions

The current native beta does not include developer-operated analytics, advertising SDKs or cross-app tracking. It does not request access to your contacts, precise location, camera or microphone. Native course data is bundled with the game. If you open an external source or information link, that website handles your visit under its own policy.

TestFlight testing

Apple operates TestFlight and processes beta-testing information. Through App Store Connect, we may receive tester information, installation and session statistics, crash diagnostics, device and operating-system information, and feedback or screenshots you submit. We use this information to investigate problems, improve the game and manage beta access. Apple describes its handling in TestFlight & Privacy.

Email and feedback

If you contact joewels99@gmail.com, we receive your email address and anything you choose to send, such as your name, device details, messages or screenshots. We use it to respond and resolve issues. Please avoid sending passwords, payment information or unrelated sensitive information. Email providers process these messages as part of delivering and storing email.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell personal information or use beta feedback for targeted advertising. Information may be handled by Apple, our email provider and service providers needed to operate testing and support, or disclosed when required by law. We retain support and beta information while needed for testing, issue resolution and related recordkeeping. Apple's retention of TestFlight data is governed by its own practices.

Your choices

You can stop testing through TestFlight and choose whether to send feedback. Removing the app removes its local app data from that device; device backups may retain separate copies. To request access, correction or deletion of information we hold, contact the email above. We will handle your request subject to applicable requirements and any records we must retain. A parent or guardian may also contact us about information a child has sent.

This policy website

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process your IP address and technical request information to deliver and secure the page, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. This page adds no analytics scripts, cookies, advertising or forms.

Changes and contact

We will update this page and its effective date if these practices change. Questions and privacy requests: joewels99@gmail.com.
